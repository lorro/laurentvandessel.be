# Really? Still here?!

So I spend most of my time working with HTML, CSS, PHP and Javascript. The last decade I mainly developed websites/applications based on the WordPress CMS or the cakePHP framework. Both are really great technologies and a real joy to work with (most of the time, that is 😄). More recently I decided to deepen my Javascript skills and started exploring Vue.js, one of my favourite JS frameworks. I really enjoy working with it and use it extensively in my day-to-day work.
